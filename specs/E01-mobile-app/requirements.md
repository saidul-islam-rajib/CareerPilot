# E01 Mobile app (Android and iOS) – requirements

Requirement IDs: §5.4, §5.5, §6, §10.1, §13, §14, §17.1, §20.2, §28, §36, §37, §41, §46.2, §47, §48, §49, §50, §53.7; acceptance criteria 72, 76, 77, 79, 80.

User stories (EARS style):

- When a new user registers, the app shall require the Terms and Privacy consent checkbox and shall record the accepted document versions.
- When the user signs in with valid credentials, the app shall store the session in secure storage and open the Today tab.
- The Today tab shall show counts (new matches, awaiting approval, applied this week, interviews), a pause banner when paused, and matches per track limited by each track's result count.
- When the user opens an opportunity, the app shall show the match explanation, hard and uncertain requirements separately, compensation, deadline, application route, and the archived circular.
- When the user prepares an application, the app shall create an approval package; when the user approves it after confirmation, the application shall be queued and completed automatically in the submission window.
- The user shall be able to edit any answer before approving; AI-drafted answers shall be marked.
- The Applications tab shall list every application with submission and outcome statuses (§20.2) and filters; the record shall show the timeline, Q&A, emails, files, evidence, and the original circular.
- The Profile tab shall show the master profile; contacts shall be reorderable by drag and drop and by accessible move buttons; photo and signature shall open in a zoomable viewer.
- The More tab shall provide track counts and modes, pause (days, months, years, until resumed), submission time window, sign-in activity, the admin-selected theme (read-only), About with the Team Sober credit, open-source licences, sign out, and account deletion with a grace period.
